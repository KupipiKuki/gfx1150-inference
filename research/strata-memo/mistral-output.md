## Decision Memo

### Recommendation

Recommendation: **Hybrid Approach (Hybrid-A)**

The Hybrid-A approach combines the strengths of the current project's evidence/claims substrate with an existing engine's compute/backends, and writes only the differentiating scheduler/residency layer. This approach leverages the proven capabilities of the current project while minimizing the development effort by utilizing an established engine.
