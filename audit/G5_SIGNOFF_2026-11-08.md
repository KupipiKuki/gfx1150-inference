## 2. Blockers closed

- **BLOCKER-7E-1 (sync count):** `LayerGuard` is armed only while an exception
  is unwinding (`std::uncaught_exceptions`); `boundary_syncs`/`drain_syncs` are
  real counters. Warm layer = **1** `hipStreamSynchronize`; cold growth drains
  are counted and documented. The trace/test/`sync-count.log` are measurement
  based, not literals.
- **BLOCKER-7E-2 (allocation failure):** all growth paths allocate the fresh
  buffer first, free the old only after success; destructor drains are guarded.
  No dangling/double-free path.
- **BLOCKER-7E-3 (CI/vacuous test):** source-contract test scans the real
  `src/kernels` directory and asserts the `.hip` files; CPU/clean-clone CI green.

## 3. Carry-forwards / QFs (non-blocking)

- **QAQC-7E-1 (closed):** workspace/KV destructor drains are try/catch guarded.
- **PTDS lane note:** `PYTHONPATH=build-ptds pytest` is masked by the conftest
  prepending `./build`; the dedicated `ptds_probe.py` with module path/sha is
  the valid PTDS evidence. Do not claim a full-suite PTDS lane.
- **Pinned slab:** the live staging path is the per-slot pinned slab
  (`hipHostMalloc`); streamed tensor views are 32-byte aligned, so
  `hipHostRegister` rarely fires. Documented; no pageable-async claim.
- **Overlap:** measured only on the larger synthetic prefill shapes; no
  throughput claim. 7F-3 owns real benchmarks.
- **Row 15:** stays NOT YET with the measured per-layer boundary
  (one sync/layer, N/token); no one-sync/token claim.
- Real 70B/8-hour/benchmark evidence remains 7F-2/7F-3/7F-4.
