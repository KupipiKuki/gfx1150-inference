## Root cause

Runner 2.338.2 checks IsConfigured() (local .runner/.credentials) before
it looks at --replace. --replace only replaces the server-side runner,
so a server-side deletion leaves the local config and config.sh refuses
with: Cannot configure the runner because it is already configured.

## Fix

ensure_runner_registered.sh now stops the service and removes
.runner, .credentials and .credentials_rsaparams before running
config.sh --replace. Regression: test_repair_removes_the_stale_local_config.
