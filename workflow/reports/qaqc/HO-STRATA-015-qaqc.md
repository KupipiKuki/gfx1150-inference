## 2. Explicit technical judgments required by QAQC brief

### 2.1 (a) Independent judgment on DEC-046: does teacher forcing measure what S4 must prove, is the gate change justified by measurement rather than a loosened gate in disguise, and is the free-running failure reported rather than hidden?

**Judgment: Yes on all counts. DEC-046 is rigorously justified by measurement; in fact, QAQC's independent recomputation from the exact-f32 oracle proves the router tie at `(layer=19, pos=1)` is ~83–160× tighter than the Q4_K-node figure cited in the coder's prose, making a free-running 5e-3 layer comparison mathematically incapable of distinguishing exact layer arithmetic from a single-ULP-scale upstream perturbation.**

1. **What S4 exists to prove vs what S5 exists to prove:**
   In the staged test plan (`planning/strata-engine/13-family-support-test-plan.md` §4), S4 (layer parity) exists to verify that each transformer block implements the exact mathematical operator of the reference graph (RMSNorm, Q/K/V projections, NORM-style RoPE, causal MHA, router logits + softmax over 64 experts, top-6 selection with `norm_w = false`, 64 routed SwiGLU experts, 2 always-active shared experts summed inside the FFN branch, and residual connections), whereas S5 (`HO-STRATA-016`) tests end-to-end multi-step token generation and KL divergence on the shipped Q4_K_M pack.
2. **Forensic verification of the `(layer=19, pos=1)` divergence:**
   QAQC independently dumped `ffn_inp-19`, `ffn_out-19`, `Kcur-19`, and `Vcur-19` from `oracle_nodes` (at llama.cpp pin `78e05298…`) on both the exact-f32 reference pack (`/tmp/opencode/deepseek-moe-16b-f32.gguf`) and the Q4_K_M pack, and compared them against the engine's free-running and teacher-forced runs:
   - Through layers `0..18`, the free-running engine tracks the exact-f32 oracle at `≤ 2.24e-4` across all 4 positions (`artifacts/ac5b_free.log`). Entering layer 19 at position 1, the inherited residual drift is `rel L2 = 6.834360e-05`; after layer 19's attention block, `ffn_inp` at `(19, 1)` still matches the exact-f32 oracle's `oracle_node_ffn_inp-19.bin[1]` to **`rel L2 = 7.123123e-05`**.
   - At `(layer=19, pos=1)`, the router computes `softmax(ffn_gate_inp^T @ rmsnorm(ffn_inp))` and picks the top 6 of 64 experts. In both the oracle and the engine, the top 5 experts are `[25, 2, 3, 22, 5]`, and 6th vs 7th place is a near-tie between **expert 40** and **expert 33**:
     - **Exact-f32 oracle (`oracle_node_ffn_inp-19.bin[1]`):** expert 40 is 6th (`logit = +1.264928e+00`, `prob = 4.224125e-02`) and expert 33 is 7th (`logit = +1.264849e+00`, `prob = 4.223790e-02`) — a gap of **`Δprob = 3.36e-6` (`Δlogit = 7.95e-5`)**.
     - **Teacher-forced engine (`artifacts/runs/teacher_run.log:98-100`):** expert 40 is 6th (`logit = +1.264890e+00`, `prob = 4.223974e-02`) and expert 33 is 7th (`logit = +1.264849e+00`, `prob = 4.223800e-02`) — a gap of **`Δprob = 1.74e-6` (`Δlogit = 4.10e-5`)**, selecting the oracle's exact top-6 `[25, 2, 3, 22, 5, 40]` and reproducing the oracle's layer-19 output to **`rel L2 = 2.372101e-05`**.
     - **Free-running engine (`free_diag.log`):** the `7.12e-5` inherited drift entering the router nudges the two logits by `~5e-5` — exceeding the `4.10e-5 … 7.95e-5` logit gap — so expert 33 becomes 6th (`logit = +1.264901e+00`, `prob = 4.224070e-02`) and expert 40 becomes 7th (`logit = +1.264841e+00`, `prob = 4.223814e-02`, gap `Δprob = 2.56e-6`, `Δlogit = 6.00e-5`), selecting `[25, 2, 3, 22, 5, 33]`.
     - *(Note on the `2.80e-4` / `6.62e-3` numbers in the coder's prose: QAQC traced `Δprob = 2.8038e-4` and `Δlogit = 6.6176e-3`, with `p(40) = 0.04251` vs `p(33) = 0.04223`, to evaluating the router on `ffn_inp-19[1]` from the **Q4_K_M** oracle rather than the exact-f32 oracle; see Minor finding **F2**.)*
   - Because top-k selection is a discontinuous step function, swapping expert 40 (`weight ≈ 0.04224`) for expert 33 (`weight ≈ 0.04224`) changes `ffn_out` by `rel L2 = 1.253243e-01` and the layer-19 residual `l_out` by `rel L2 = 2.668110e-02`.
3. **Why teacher forcing is sharper, not looser:**
   Widening the free-running bound from `5e-3` to `> 6.32e-2` would allow a broken MoE block (e.g., wrong shared-expert scaling or missing an expert) to pass across all 28 layers. Under DEC-046, the bound stays `5e-3` and every single one of the 112 `(layer, position)` cells is tested from the reference's exact pre-block residual and prior-position K/V cache, achieving a worst-case error of **`1.569942e-04` at `(layer=4, pos=3)`** (>31× margin below `5e-3`). Furthermore, the free-running failure is not hidden: `artifacts/runs/s4b-free-normal.bin` and `artifacts/ac5b_free.log` (ending in `RESULT: FAIL`) are committed, hashed, and explained in `manifest.md` §§1, 2, 4 and `artifacts/llama-ref-moe.md`.
