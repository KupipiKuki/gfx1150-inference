## Notes

- **No-arithmetic-change proof:** `ds_f32_matvec.cu/.cuh`, `ds_kernels.cu/.cuh`
  carry **identical digests** at the base `e2e7e1b347…` and the final
  `3c2f89555a…` — the kernels that produced S4's 1.569942e-04 were not touched.
  Independently, all six engine logits dumps re-captured after the block-0
  assertion (`3c2f89555`) are byte-identical to the pre-assertion captures.
- The signed `build-hip/strata` (`d58ee239…`) was never rebuilt or modified; it
  is the regression baseline for HO-007 and the AC-8 host (guard rail).
- Greedy sampling is deterministic (argmax; ties → lowest token id), documented
  in `src/deepseek/ds_engine.cu:548` at the pinned commit (AC-5 evidence).
- `scripts/oracle_s5.cpp` is derived from the committed
  `HO-STRATA-014/scripts/oracle_nodes.cpp` (parent SHA256
  `25fe94526644c24bed527c02b25c1cff30247e3ceeafc741b7f6eaaa733942ca`,
  recorded in the source header). The harness never detokenizes;
  `--tokens` are raw ids from the HO-010 parity table.
- The oracle binary and the llama-ref library tree are environment, not repo
  state; rebuild is pinned by the llama.cpp commit
  `78e05298f0d68abff41d08d79fa7469264b2aff0` (`/tmp/opencode/llama-ref`) plus
  the committed source above.
